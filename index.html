      How Sige works

      The one rule

      You can only write at the end of your draft. Text you have finished is locked, and you cannot click or arrow back into it. Scroll up to read. You can select and copy any text, but you cannot edit it. Then keep writing, and revise later in another app.

      Settings choose the writing frontier: text locks at the character, word, sentence, or paragraph boundary. Anything behind that frontier is history and cannot be undone. Enter always starts a new paragraph.

      Tags

      Type a tag anywhere in your draft to leave yourself a note and keep going. Add detail after a colon, for example [TK: year the harbor closed].

      • [TK] a fact, name, or number to add later
      • [source] something to cite or check
      • [word] a word you can't find yet
      • [fix] something earlier is wrong
      • [cont] a continuity problem
      • [note] any other thought. You can also add up to three tags of your own in Settings.

      Titles

      Start a paragraph with # and a space to make it a bold title. A title locks when you press Enter, and the # then disappears from the screen. The # stays in the export, and the first title names your export file. Start a paragraph with ## for a subheading. Type *** alone on a line and press Enter for a scene break.

      Notes and shortcuts

      • +J (or Alt+J) opens the notes panel with a new note ready. Press Enter to save and keep writing.
      • Click outside the panel to close it. In the panel you can edit any note.
      • +S opens Export. +, opens settings.
      • +Z undoes your most recent action while it is still inside the writing frontier. Undo never reopens locked text.
      • +I starts italics and shows the closing asterisk after your cursor. To step past the closing asterisk and keep writing in plain text, press the Right Arrow key or End, press it again, or type *. If you press Enter first, the closing asterisk is added for you. Pressing it twice in a row cancels.
      • When you type [ a faint completion appears. Press Tab to accept it. You can also finish a tag by typing its closing bracket yourself.
      • +Shift+Enter finishes the draft and locks all of it.
      • Type -- for a dash.

      Export

      A clean export is the manuscript without Sige notes or tags. A revision Markdown export keeps the manuscript clean and adds a human-readable Revision notes section with each note's type, creation time, paragraph, and nearby context. Export is the handoff from writing forward to revising in another environment.

      Export draft

      Choose what to take into revision. Clean exports contain only the manuscript. Revision exports carry the unresolved notes with it.

      Finish this draft?

      Everything locks, and you cannot add more to this draft. You can still read it, copy it, and export it. Start a new document to write something else.

      Start a new document?

      The current draft goes to your shelf, so you can export it later. Erasing it instead deletes it from this browser.
